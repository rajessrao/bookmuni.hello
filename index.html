bookmuni For communities

That book on your shelf? Someone two streets away would love to borrow it.

bookmuni helps people in the same building, campus, or club lend and borrow physical books with neighbors they already know — not strangers on a marketplace.

We'll only write to you once there's a community open near you.

Run a community instead? See how below.

How lending actually works

1

List what's on your shelf

Add the books you own and mark them available to your community — condition, genre, whatever people need to know before asking.

2

Someone nearby borrows it

A member requests it, you approve, and you agree on a simple pickup — usually a spot your community already uses.

3

It comes home on time

Reminders keep the loan on track, and a quick rating after return keeps the whole community trustworthy.

Built for a community you already trust

Private by invitation

Every community is closed — you join with an invite code and admin approval, not a public signup anyone can find.

No marketplace, no payments

Books move between neighbors, not strangers, and nothing here is bought, sold, or delivered.

Real accountability

Every loan is tracked from pickup to return, with reminders and lightweight ratings — not a spreadsheet or an honor system.
